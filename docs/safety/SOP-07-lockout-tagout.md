# SOP-07 Lockout/Tagout (Hazardous Energy Control)

## 1 Purpose and Scope

This procedure protects employees from unexpected energization, start-up, or release of stored energy while servicing or maintaining machines. It applies to all authorized employees at Riverside Plant performing maintenance on equipment including pumps (P-101, P-102), conveyors (CNV-1 to CNV-4), the stamping press (PRS-2), and air compressors (CMP-1).

## 2 Responsibilities

- **Authorized employee:** applies and removes their own lock and tag. Only the person who applied a lock may remove it.
- **Affected employee:** operates the machine but does not service it. Must be notified before and after lockout.
- **Shift supervisor:** verifies compliance, maintains the lock log, and approves group lockouts.

## 3 Lockout Procedure

### 3.1 Required PPE

| Task | Required PPE |
|---|---|
| Electrical isolation (480V panels) | Arc-rated face shield, Class 0 gloves, safety glasses, FR clothing |
| Mechanical/pneumatic isolation | Safety glasses, cut-resistant gloves, steel-toe boots |
| Hydraulic bleed-down (PRS-2) | Face shield, chemical-resistant gloves, safety glasses |

### 3.2 Steps to Apply Lockout

1. Notify all affected employees that the machine will be shut down and locked out.
2. Identify all energy sources using the equipment's Energy Isolation Placard (electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, gravity, thermal).
3. Shut down the machine using normal stopping controls.
4. Operate all energy-isolating devices (disconnects, valves) to the OFF/closed position.
5. Apply your personal lock and a completed danger tag to each isolating device.
6. Release or restrain stored energy: bleed air lines, lower or block raised parts, discharge capacitors, bleed hydraulic accumulators.
7. Verify isolation: attempt to start the machine using normal controls, then return controls to OFF.
8. Test electrical conductors with a rated voltmeter (live-dead-live check) before touching.

### 3.3 Removing Lockout

1. Inspect the work area; remove tools and reinstall all guards.
2. Ensure all employees are clear and notify affected employees.
3. Each authorized employee removes only their own lock and tag.
4. Re-energize and confirm normal operation.

## 4 Special Situations

### 4.1 Group Lockout

When more than one employee services the same machine, a group lockbox is used. The shift supervisor applies the master lock; each worker attaches a personal lock to the lockbox. The master lock is removed last.

### 4.2 Lock Removal When Employee Is Absent

Only the shift supervisor may remove an absent employee's lock, after (a) verifying the employee is not on site, (b) attempting to contact them, and (c) documenting the removal in the lock log. The employee must be informed before resuming work.

### 4.3 Minor Servicing Exception

Clearing a jam on CNV-1 to CNV-4 may be done without full lockout **only** if the conveyor's E-stop is engaged, the jam can be cleared with the provided hook tool from outside the guard, and no body part enters the danger zone. Reaching inside the guard always requires full lockout. Bypassing light curtains or interlocks is never permitted.

## 5 Training and Audit

Authorized employees receive LOTO training at hire and annually. The safety coordinator audits each energy control procedure at least once per year.
