# SOP-03 Personal Protective Equipment by Area

## 1 Purpose

This procedure defines minimum PPE for each work area at Riverside Plant. Supervisors may require additional PPE based on a job hazard analysis.

## 2 PPE Matrix

| Area | Equipment | Eyes | Hands | Hearing | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Line 2 Coolant System | P-101, P-102 | Safety glasses | Chemical-resistant gloves | Not required | Steel-toe boots |
| Line 1 Conveyors | CNV-1 to CNV-4 | Safety glasses | Cut-resistant gloves | Required above 85 dB | No loose clothing, hair tied back |
| Stamping Bay | PRS-2 | Safety glasses with side shields | Cut-resistant gloves | Double protection required | Metatarsal boots |
| Line 3 Welding | WLD-1, WLD-2 | Welding helmet shade 10 | Leather welding gloves | Ear plugs | FR jacket, respirator for stainless |
| Compressor Room | CMP-1 | Safety glasses | Work gloves | Required | Steel-toe boots |

## 3 Rules

### 3.1 Hearing Protection

Hearing protection is mandatory wherever noise reaches 85 dB(A) averaged over 8 hours. The stamping bay near PRS-2 measures 98 dB(A) at peak, so earplugs plus earmuffs are required. Hearing tests are offered every year.

### 3.2 Inspecting and Replacing PPE

1. Inspect PPE before every use for cracks, tears, or contamination.
2. Tag damaged PPE out of service and return it to the safety store.
3. Collect replacement PPE from the safety store and sign the issue log.

### 3.3 Task-Specific Additions

Electrical isolation work on 480V panels requires arc-rated PPE as listed in SOP-07. Work on the CMP-1 discharge line during hot operation requires thermal gloves because surfaces reach 90 C.
