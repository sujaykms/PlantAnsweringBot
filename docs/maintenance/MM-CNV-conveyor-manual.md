# MM-CNV Conveyor CNV-3 Maintenance Manual

## 1 Equipment Overview

Conveyors CNV-1 to CNV-4 are belt conveyors on Line 1. CNV-3 carries finished stampings from PRS-2 to packing. Each has a 3 HP gear motor, a 600 mm wide PVC belt, and a drive roller with tracking sensors. Criticality: medium.

## 2 Fault Codes

| Fault Code | Description | Likely Cause | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| F01 | Belt mistracking | Uneven tension or worn idler | Adjust take-up bolts; check idlers |
| F02 | Motor overcurrent | Jam or overloaded belt | Clear the jam; reduce load |
| F03 | Belt slip detected | Low tension or worn lagging | Re-tension belt; inspect drive lagging |
| F07 | Light curtain tripped | Obstruction at the guard | Clear obstruction; do not bypass the curtain |
| F09 | Gearbox over-temperature | Low oil or bearing wear | Check oil level; inspect bearings |

## 3 Preventive Maintenance Schedule

| Task | Interval | Est. Time | Parts / Consumables |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visual check of belt, idlers, and guards | 7 days | 10 min | None |
| Check belt tension and tracking | 30 days | 20 min | None |
| Grease idler bearings | 90 days | 30 min | Lithium EP2 grease |
| Change gearbox oil | 365 days | 60 min | ISO VG 220 gear oil, 2.5 L |
| Inspect drive lagging | 180 days | 30 min | None |

## 4 Belt Replacement

Full lockout per SOP-07 is required before any work. Reaching inside the guard always requires lockout, even to clear a jam.

1. Notify operators and stop CNV-3 using the normal stop control.
2. Apply lockout/tagout to the CNV-3 motor disconnect per SOP-07 and verify zero energy.
3. Remove the side guards and release belt tension at the take-up bolts.
4. Unfasten the belt splice and pull the old belt out.
5. Thread the new belt, matching the arrow direction on the belt underside.
6. Fasten the splice with new clips and check the joint is square.
7. Re-tension the belt until deflection is 10 mm under 5 kg load at mid-span.
8. Reinstall guards, remove locks per SOP-07, and run empty for 10 minutes to check tracking.

|---|---|---|
