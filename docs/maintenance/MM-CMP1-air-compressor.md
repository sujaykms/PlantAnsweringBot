# MM-CMP1 Air Compressor CMP-1 Maintenance Manual

## 1 Equipment Overview

CMP-1 is a 50 HP rotary screw compressor supplying 125 psi shop air to Line 1 and the stamping press PRS-2. It has a 500 L air receiver. Loss of CMP-1 stops the PRS-2 clutch and brake. Criticality: high.

## 2 Preventive Maintenance Schedule

| Task | Interval | Est. Time | Parts / Consumables |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drain condensate from receiver | 1 day | 5 min | None |
| Replace air intake filter | 2000 hours | 30 min | Filter element AF-50 |
| Replace oil filter | 2000 hours | 30 min | Oil filter OF-50 |
| Replace separator element | 4000 hours | 60 min | Separator SE-50 |
| Change compressor oil | 4000 hours | 90 min | Synthetic compressor oil, 20 L |
| Test safety relief valve | 365 days | 30 min | None |

## 3 Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fault Code | Likely Cause | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| High discharge temperature | C12 | Dirty cooler or low oil | Clean cooler; check oil level |
| Low pressure at PRS-2 | C20 | Clogged intake filter or leaks | Replace filter AF-50; check for leaks |
| High oil carryover | C31 | Saturated separator element | Replace separator SE-50 |
| Frequent loading cycles | C40 | Air leaks | Perform leak survey |

## 4 Air Intake Filter Replacement

Lockout/tagout per SOP-07 is required, including bleeding the receiver. The compressor stores pneumatic energy that must be released.

1. Notify the shift supervisor that PRS-2 will lose shop air.
2. Stop CMP-1 and apply lockout/tagout to the CMP-1 main disconnect per SOP-07.
3. Close the receiver outlet valve and open the drain valve to bleed the pressure to 0 psi.
4. Open the intake housing cover and remove the old element AF-50.
5. Wipe the housing clean and check the seal for cracks.
6. Install the new element and close the housing; torque the cover clamps to 12 N·m.
7. Remove locks per SOP-07, restart CMP-1, and confirm pressure reaches 125 psi with no leaks.
8. Reset the filter service hour counter on the controller.
