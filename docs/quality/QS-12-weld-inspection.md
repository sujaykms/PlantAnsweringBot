# QS-12 Weld Seam Inspection Standard

## 1 Scope

Applies to all MIG-welded seams on bracket assemblies BRK-200 and BRK-210 produced on Line 3 robotic weld cells (WLD-1, WLD-2). Defines acceptance criteria, sampling plans, and nonconformance handling.

## 2 Acceptance Criteria

| Part | Characteristic | Nominal | Lower Limit | Upper Limit | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BRK-200 | Fillet weld leg size | 6.0 | 5.5 | 7.0 | mm |
| BRK-200 | Weld length | 40 | 38 | 44 | mm |
| BRK-200 | Surface porosity (max pore diameter) | — | — | 1.0 | mm |
| BRK-210 | Fillet weld leg size | 8.0 | 7.5 | 9.0 | mm |
| BRK-210 | Undercut depth | — | — | 0.5 | mm |
| BRK-210 | Surface porosity (pores per 100 mm) | — | — | 2 | count |

Cracks of any size, incomplete fusion, and burn-through are rejectable defects with zero tolerance.

## 3 Sampling Plan

Sampling follows ANSI/ASQ Z1.4, general inspection level II, normal inspection.

| Lot Size | Sample Size | AQL 1.0 Accept / Reject |
|---|---|---|
| 91–150 | 20 | 0 / 1 |
| 151–280 | 32 | 1 / 2 |
| 281–500 | 50 | 1 / 2 |
| 501–1200 | 80 | 2 / 3 |

Visual inspection is performed on every lot. Destructive macro-etch testing is performed on 1 part per shift per weld cell.

## 4 Statistical Process Control

Fillet leg size is charted on an X-bar/R chart with subgroups of 5 parts every 2 hours. React to any of the following signals:

1. Any single point outside the control limits.
2. Seven consecutive points on the same side of the center line.
3. Six consecutive points steadily increasing or decreasing.
4. Two of three consecutive points beyond 2 sigma on the same side.

On a signal, the operator stops the cell, notifies the quality technician, and checks wire feed speed, contact tip wear, and fixture clamping before restarting.

## 5 Nonconformance Handling

1. Tag the lot with a red HOLD tag and move it to the quarantine cage.
2. Open a nonconformance report (NCR) in the QMS within 2 hours, listing part number, lot number, defect, and quantity.
3. The quality engineer dispositions the lot: rework, use-as-is (requires customer approval), or scrap.
4. Reworked parts are re-inspected at 100% before release.
5. If the same defect recurs on 3 lots within 30 days, a corrective action (8D) is required.
