# MM-P101 Centrifugal Pump P-101 Maintenance Manual

## 1 Equipment Overview

Pump P-101 is a horizontal end-suction centrifugal pump supplying coolant to Line 2 machining cells. Rated 250 GPM at 120 ft head, driven by a 15 HP, 480V, 1750 RPM TEFC motor. Standby pump P-102 is identical and shares this manual. Criticality: high (Line 2 stops without coolant).

## 2 Preventive Maintenance Schedule

| Task | Interval | Est. Time | Parts / Consumables |
|---|---|---|---|
| Check vibration and bearing temperature | 7 days | 10 min | None |
| Inspect mechanical seal for leakage | 7 days | 5 min | None |
| Grease motor bearings | 90 days | 20 min | Polyrex EM grease, 15 g per bearing |
| Check coupling alignment | 180 days | 60 min | Shim kit SK-15 |
| Replace mechanical seal | 365 days or on leak | 180 min | Seal kit MS-101-A, O-ring set OR-101 |
| Motor insulation resistance test | 365 days | 30 min | None |

## 3 Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fault Code | Likely Cause | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low discharge pressure | E21 | Clogged suction strainer | Clean strainer ST-101 |
| High vibration (> 0.3 in/s) | E34 | Misalignment or worn bearings | Check alignment (Section 5); inspect bearings |
| Seal leakage > 10 drops/min | E35 | Worn mechanical seal | Replace seal (Section 4) |
| Motor trips on overload | E42 | Impeller rubbing or blocked discharge | Check discharge valve; inspect impeller clearance |
| Pump runs but no flow | E50 | Loss of prime / air in suction | Vent casing, check suction line for leaks |

## 4 Mechanical Seal Replacement

Lockout/tagout per SOP-07 is required before starting. Switch duty to P-102 first.

1. Switch coolant duty to standby pump P-102 and confirm Line 2 flow.
2. Apply lockout/tagout to P-101 motor disconnect MCC-2-07 per SOP-07.
3. Close suction valve V-101S and discharge valve V-101D; drain casing to the coolant sump.
4. Remove coupling guard and coupling spacer.
5. Unbolt and remove the back pull-out assembly (8 × M16 casing bolts).
6. Remove impeller nut (left-hand thread) and impeller.
7. Remove old seal; clean shaft sleeve and inspect for scoring (replace sleeve if wear > 0.1 mm).
8. Install new seal kit MS-101-A with fresh O-rings; lubricate faces with clean coolant only.
9. Reinstall impeller; torque impeller nut to 95 N·m.
10. Reinstall back pull-out assembly; torque casing bolts in a star pattern to 135 N·m.
11. Reinstall coupling spacer and guard; check alignment (Section 5).
12. Open valves, vent casing, remove lockout per SOP-07, and run for 15 minutes checking for leaks.
