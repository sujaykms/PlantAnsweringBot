# QS-20 Dimensional Inspection of Stamped Parts from PRS-2

## 1 Scope

Applies to bracket blanks BRK-200 and BRK-210 stamped on press PRS-2 before welding at WLD-1. Covers dimensional checks, burr limits, and the link between defects and die condition.

## 2 Acceptance Criteria

| Part | Characteristic | Nominal | Lower Limit | Upper Limit | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BRK-200 | Overall length | 120.0 | 119.7 | 120.3 | mm |
| BRK-200 | Hole diameter | 10.0 | 9.95 | 10.10 | mm |
| BRK-200 | Edge burr height | — | — | 0.15 | mm |
| BRK-210 | Overall length | 150.0 | 149.6 | 150.4 | mm |
| BRK-210 | Flatness | — | — | 0.30 | mm |
| BRK-210 | Edge burr height | — | — | 0.20 | mm |

## 3 Defects and Probable Causes

### 3.1 Burrs

Burr height above the limit is usually caused by die wear or excessive punch-to-die clearance. A burr trend that increases over a run indicates a worn die edge on PRS-2. Burrs also cause poor fit-up and weld porosity at WLD-1, so excess burrs are a quality risk downstream.

### 3.2 Dimensional Drift

Length drift is typically caused by worn die guide pins or a loose die setting. Hole diameter growth indicates a worn punch.

## 4 Inspection Procedure

1. Take 5 consecutive parts at the start of each shift and after every die change.
2. Measure length and hole diameter with a calibrated caliper.
3. Measure burr height with the profile gauge on all four edges.
4. Record the results in the SPC chart per QS-05.
5. If any burr height exceeds the limit, stop PRS-2 and raise a maintenance request for die inspection.
6. Quarantine all parts since the last good sample.

## 5 Disposition

Parts with burrs up to 0.30 mm may be deburred and re-inspected. Parts with dimensional failures are scrapped. Die maintenance on PRS-2 requires lockout per SOP-07, including hydraulic bleed-down.
