# SOP-12 Confined Space Entry

## 1 Purpose and Scope

This procedure controls entry into permit-required confined spaces at Riverside Plant, including the Line 2 coolant sump (below pump P-101), the CMP-1 air receiver tank, and the quench tank at WLD-1. A confined space has limited entry and exit, is not designed for continuous occupancy, and may contain hazardous atmosphere.

## 2 Roles

- **Entry supervisor:** signs the entry permit and cancels it when work ends.
- **Attendant:** stays outside, monitors the entrant, and never enters the space. Calls rescue if needed.
- **Entrant:** performs the work and leaves immediately on any alarm.

## 3 Entry Procedure

### 3.1 Atmospheric Limits

| Gas | Acceptable Range | Action if Outside Range |
|---|---|---|
| Oxygen | 19.5% to 23.5% | Do not enter; ventilate and retest |
| Flammable gas (LEL) | Below 10% of LEL | Do not enter; ventilate and retest |
| Carbon monoxide | Below 25 ppm | Do not enter; find the source |
| Hydrogen sulfide | Below 10 ppm | Do not enter; ventilate and retest |

### 3.2 Steps to Enter a Confined Space

1. Obtain a confined space entry permit from the shift supervisor.
2. Isolate all energy sources to connected equipment. Lockout/tagout per SOP-07 is mandatory for pumps (P-101, P-102), mixers, and compressors (CMP-1) feeding the space.
3. Blank or double-block-and-bleed all connected piping.
4. Ventilate the space with a forced-air blower for at least 15 minutes.
5. Test the atmosphere with a calibrated 4-gas meter at top, middle, and bottom, in the order oxygen, flammables, toxics.
6. Post an attendant at the entrance and set up the retrieval tripod and harness.
7. Entrant dons required PPE and harness, then enters. Continuous gas monitoring stays on throughout.
8. On completion, all personnel exit, the attendant counts heads, and the entry supervisor cancels the permit.

### 3.3 Emergency Rescue

Never enter to rescue. The attendant calls Plant Emergency at ext. 911 and uses the retrieval line to pull out the entrant. Only the trained rescue team may make an entry rescue.

## 4 Permit Rules

Permits are valid for one shift only. Hot work inside a confined space needs a separate hot work permit. Any change in conditions voids the permit.
